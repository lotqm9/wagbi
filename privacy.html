Privacy

Last updated November 2, 2026

What you type and the coins you make

When you make a coin, wagbi stores what you typed, the pictures you attached, the links you shared, wagbi's replies and the coin it made (the name, ticker, bio, logo, banner and website) so you can come back to it and keep changing it. Every coin made on wagbi is public, and so is what was stored with it: it shows on the coins tab and has its own page and website.

Your chats

Your chat history is kept in your browser. If you sign in with Google, it is also kept on our servers under your account so it follows you to other devices. "Clear all chat history" in settings deletes it from this device and from your account.

Signing in with Google

When you sign in, Google tells us your name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to show who is signed in and to keep your chats. Your account is stored under a one-way hash, not under your Google ID.

Voice input

When you use the microphone, the recording is sent to a transcription service (OpenAI or ElevenLabs) to turn it into text for the prompt box. We don't keep the recording.

Launching a coin

To launch, you send SOL to a one-time deposit address. Its key is kept encrypted until the launch is done and any leftover SOL is sent back. The coin's address, its payout wallets and its fee split are public, on the blockchain and on the coin's page.

Stopping abuse

To apply rate limits, we keep a one-way hash of your IP address for about an hour.

Services that handle your data

Anthropic (Claude) writes and draws the coins, and image generation services may help with the art. Vercel hosts the site and stores its data. Google handles sign-in. OpenAI or ElevenLabs transcribe voice. Solana RPC providers send launch transactions to the network.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, and the site has no ad trackers.
