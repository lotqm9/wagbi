What wagbi is

wagbi uses AI to make a coin from a sentence: its name, ticker, art and words. You can keep changing it in the chat, and launch it on a Solana launchpad when it looks right.

What you make is yours to check

AI can get things wrong. Look over everything before you launch or post it. You are responsible for the coins you launch and for what you say about them.

What you can't make

Don't use wagbi to make coins that pretend to be a real person, company or brand, or that are hateful, harassing or illegal. We can remove any coin from wagbi.

Launching is real

A launch creates a real token on a public blockchain, and it can't be undone. Nothing on wagbi is financial advice. Coins can lose all of their value.

Fees

Each launchpad shows its fees before you pay. On pump.fun, wagbi takes 15% of the coin's creator fees and the rest goes to the wallets you choose; the split is locked at launch. On the other launchpads, you keep the creator fees. Network and launchpad fees apply everywhere.

Deposits and refunds

You pay for a launch by sending SOL to a one-time deposit address. Never send from an exchange. Whatever is left after the launch goes back to your payout wallet. If a launch fails, what you sent is refunded, minus any network fees already spent.

No promises

wagbi is provided as it is, without warranties. We aren't liable for losses from using it, from launches, or from the market.
